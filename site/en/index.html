Getting started

Start cleaning up Downloads.

Install the app, review the names to change,
and start automatic cleanup in Downloads.

STEP 01

Open the installer

Open the downloaded .dmg, double-click Install Jaso NFC.app, and choose Install.

When it finishes, keep the installer file or move it to Trash. Close the installer window and eject the mounted disk in Finder.

STEP 02

Review the files to rename

Choose Jaso NFC in the menu bar → Manage folders…. Pick your folders and choose Preview filenames to compare current and proposed names side by side.

Start with Downloads, then add other folders you need or remove them from the list.

STEP 03

Start automatic cleanup

After reviewing the preview, choose Start automatic cleanup. Jaso NFC cleans up your chosen folders and keeps checking new files.

Use Open Status… to see current activity and items that need attention.

Folder settings

Made for everyday use

Your everyday controls,
in the menu bar.

Jaso NFC keeps watching your chosen folders. Check how cleanup is going, pause for another task, and resume when you are ready.

  • Open StatusSee current activity and waiting items. Each problem includes the file path and steps to check it.
  • Pause / ResumePut cleanup on hold while you focus on another task. Choose Resume to process the waiting work.
  • Manage foldersUpdate your folder list and preview the names to change.
  • SettingsSet the app to run at login, then choose your language, font style, and icon animation.
  • Close / QuitClose the window to leave cleanup running in the background. Choose Quit Jaso NFC to stop it too. Next time, open the app and choose Start worker.

A few things to know

Your questions.

Find the steps you need,
from choosing folders to solving problems.

How do I allow the app to open?

After checking that you trust the download, follow Apple’s app opening instructions. Try opening the app, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If that option is unavailable or you see a damage message, download it again or contact support.

How do I clean up another folder?

Open Manage folders… to add a folder, preview the names, and save your settings. To recheck a folder already in your selection, open Status and choose Recheck all watched roots. Follow the folder setup guide for the steps.

What should I do when a cloud folder is waiting to retry?

Check the path and reason in Status, then try opening the folder in Finder. Also check the login and connection in OneDrive, Google Drive, or your sync app. Once access is restored, Jaso NFC can try again from the scheduled retry time.

Which names does the app clean up?

Jaso NFC processes file and folder names that need NFC normalization, including decomposed Korean, within your chosen folders. Separately typed compatibility letters such as ㅎㅏㄴ are outside this conversion. See the processing scope guide for automatic exclusions, such as selected app and photo-library formats, and folders you can exclude yourself.

How do I undo name changes or remove the app?

Stop the worker, then follow the recovery and removal steps. Retained history can restore earlier names when the original files can still be identified. Later moves or replacements may limit recovery. On exFAT, simultaneous renaming by another app can create an overwrite risk; see the processing limits in the guide before using it there.

Where can I find the processing history?

Choose Open recovery history in Status to see the name-change records stored on your Mac. To ask for help with a problem, use Copy diagnostics to share its status. Remove private filenames and paths before sharing.
