Getting started

Start with Downloads.

Install the app, review the proposed names,
and start automatic cleanup in your chosen folders.

STEP 01

Open the installer

Open the downloaded .dmg, double-click Install Jaso NFC.app, and choose Install.

Keep the original DMG or move it to Trash when finished. Close the installer and eject its mounted disk in Finder.

STEP 02

Review folders and names

Choose Open Jaso NFC… from the menu bar, then select Folders. Choose Preview filenames to compare current and proposed names.

Start with Downloads, then add other folders and external drives you need.

STEP 03

Start automatic cleanup

After reviewing the result, choose Start automatic cleanup. Jaso NFC keeps checking your chosen folders and new files.

If you paused earlier, choose Resume. Follow progress in Status and Activity.

Folders and drives

Choose where cleanup happens.

Manage folders and exclusions in Folders. For each drive, choose whether cleanup resumes automatically or starts manually when reconnected.

in one window.

Choose Open Jaso NFC… from the menu bar to return. Five tabs help you follow progress, choose locations, and review original names.

A few things to know

Your questions.

Find the steps you need,
from choosing folders to solving problems.

How do I allow the app to open?

After checking the download source, follow Apple’s app opening instructions. Try opening the app, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If that option is unavailable or you see a damage message, download it again or contact support.

How do I pause or quit?

Use Pause and Resume in the menu to choose when cleanup runs. Closing the window leaves background work running. Choose Quit Jaso NFC to stop it too, then open the app from Applications when you want to use it again.

How do I reconnect or remove an external drive?

Select it in Folders → Drives and choose Manage selected drive…. Choose automatic resume or a manual start for each connection. The app's global pause remains in effect.

Choose Remove from list in the drive's details when you finish using it. You can remove a disconnected drive. With no other folder edits, removal is saved immediately. If your other folder edits need a preview, preview them and choose Save settings to apply the removal too. Your saved exclusion remains in effect after reconnection. See the folder and drive settings guide for details.

Can I choose hidden folders or keep an empty folder list?

Hidden folders whose names begin with a dot, such as .ghost-alice and .venv, are excluded by default. Add a hidden folder directly in Folders → Selected folders to include it. Hidden subfolders inside that selection remain excluded.

You can save an empty Selected folders list. Cleanup waits until you add another folder, so choose your locations when you are ready.

Where do I see activity and history?

Use Activity to see the current path, recent work, and items currently waiting. When a task stops, its entry shows the recorded cause, such as cloud storage or access permissions. A later successful check updates the entry with its completion result. Retries for the same issue update the count and time of its existing entry.

Each completed name change creates one History record. Search retained recent changes and filter them by date and result. Select a record to inspect its original name, changed name, and location, or restore the original name.

Can it repair garbled Korean filenames?

It repairs eligible filenames originally encoded as UTF-8 or CP949 and misread as Latin-1 or Windows-1252 when there is sufficient evidence to recover the Korean name without losing information. Review the result in Preview filenames. Names with weak evidence or multiple plausible interpretations keep their current spelling. See examples and supported cases in the filename repair guide.

How do I restore an original name?

Select a History item and choose Restore original name. Read the availability check, choose Confirm restore, and wait for the completion message. If the file moved or another item occupies the name, the app explains why. See the name restoration guide.

What should I do about waiting cloud items or low storage?

Check the path and reason in Activity, then inspect the connection and download state in Finder or your sync app. Check disk space and app data through Settings → Advanced settings → Storage usage…. Choose Open storage settings when you need to free up space.

The file index keeps the information it needs in a compact form. History and logs stay within storage limits, with older entries retired automatically. Records needed by pending recovery are kept separately.

Which names are processed, and how do I remove the app?

Jaso NFC composes decomposed Korean file and folder names and repairs eligible garbled filenames within your selection. See the scope guide for compatibility letters such as ㅎㅏㄴ, automatic exclusions, and exFAT precautions. To remove the app, turn off login startup, quit, and follow the removal steps before moving the app from Applications to Trash.
